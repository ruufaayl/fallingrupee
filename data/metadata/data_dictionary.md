| Variable | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Policy interest rate | Excluded | SBP's statistics portal refused automated access (HTTP 403) and the IMF IFS policy-rate series is not in the mirror; monetary policy works mainly through the exchange rate and demand, whose effect on food prices is partly captured by EXR. |
| Broad money (M2) | Excluded | IMF IFS FMB_XDC ends in 2025M04 (8 missing months); adding it would shorten the common sample. |

| Import prices / agricultural output / rainfall | Excluded | No consistent monthly official series for 2015–2025 was obtainable; event dummies for the 2022 floods are tested instead. |

# Team Plan — The Price of a Falling Rupee

Total talk: about 12–15 minutes (≈ 4–5 minutes each), then questions.

| Member | Part | Slides | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Member 1** | Opening & set-up | slides 1–7 | Title, problem statement, research question, dependent & independent variables, data, the equation, hypotheses H1–H6 |
| **Member 2** | Data & tests | slides 8–13 | Trend graph, descriptive statistics, correlation, stationarity (ADF), multicollinearity (VIF) |
| **Member 3** | Results & conclusion | slides 14–21 | Regression output, hypothesis decisions, autocorrelation, heteroskedasticity, normality, RESET, asymmetry, conclusion, Q&A |

**Hand-overs** — Member 1 ends with: “Now my teammate will show the data and the tests.” Member 2 ends with: “Now my teammate will present the regression results.”

**Files to bring** — the slides (`presentation/The_Price_of_a_Falling_Rupee_Deck.pptx`), the dataset (`deliverables/Falling_Rupee_Dataset.xlsx`), the website (https://the-price-of-a-falling-rupee.vercel.app).

**Rehearsal tip** — each member reads the script once aloud, then presents from the slide only. Know your numbers (see the viva guide).
